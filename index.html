Rush

Chase down the target before you run out of balls or wickets.

  • Aim: move the mouse left or right (or A / D). Left is leg side, right is off side.
  • Swing: click or press Space as the ball reaches you.
  • Lofted shot: right-click, or hold Shift / W while you swing.
  • Touch: tap where you want to hit. Tap the top half of the screen for a lofted shot.
